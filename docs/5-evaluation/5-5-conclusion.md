## 5.5 Conclusion

This project provided a challenge in developing a user-facing application for 
limited, embedded hardware. As a result, it taught me how to balance
principles of efficient software design with care for the user experience.
The nature of devices like calculators means algorithm implementation details
that are often abstracted become more apparent and important to the user, and I
believe I demonstrated this through my care in choosing how to design my program.

In addition, each element of the grapher required some sort of mathematical knowledge
that added to my perceived complexity of accomplishing the design. I had to study several
textbooks and articles on graphics, matrices in 3 dimensions and implicit rasterisation algorithms.
Bringing these ideas into fruitition by coding them into my program provided useful practical experience
on top of studying the theory.
